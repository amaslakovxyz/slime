The problem

Everything moves at AI speed.
Backends are petrified clay.

Frontends get generated in minutes. Agents reason on the fly. Then every one of them waits in line for someone to hand-write the backend it needs.

01 · VIBECODED UIsREST + DTO + deploy

The UI took ten minutes. Its backend takes two weeks.

Lovable, v0 and Cursor ship a screen before lunch. Wiring it to real data still means routes, models and a deploy for every widget.

Vibecode the UI. Let Slime be the backend.

02 · AI AGENTSraw SQL / 1 endpoint per action

Too dangerous to trust, too narrow to be useful.

Give an agent raw database access and you risk everything. Hand-code an endpoint for every action and it can barely move.

Unleash your agents. Keep your database untouchable.

03 · LEGACY SYSTEMSSOAP · BFF · glue

Every new client needs another layer of glue.

Old services and tangled schemas mean each new app or AI tool gets its own fragile middleware, and nobody wants to touch it.

Don't rewrite legacy. Let Slime digest it.

04 · SAAS CUSTOMIZATIONfork per customer

Every custom report becomes a code fork.

Enterprise customers want their own views, fields and webhooks. Each one adds a branch to maintain and eats into margins.

One runtime. Every customer's view.
